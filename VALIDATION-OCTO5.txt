Diagnostic behavior
-------------------
- Automatic on every login/reload for this diagnostic build.
- /qdiag stop pauses only the current session; it re-arms after reload/restart.
- Stored in Quiver_Diagnostics inside the ordinary Quiver.lua SavedVariables file.
- Trace is bounded and rolling; it does not upload anything.
- Players can add /qdiag mark <description> around visible bugs.

Important limitation
--------------------
These tests are static, unit, and simulated runtime validation. They do not replace
real OctoWoW gameplay across every hunter build, weapon, macro, latency/FPS condition,
locale, raid state, and server edge case. The retained diagnostic recorder is intended
to capture those remaining real-client cases from players before declaring a final
non-diagnostic baseline.
